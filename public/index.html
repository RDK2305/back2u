How CampusFind works

Three steps to reunite with your lost belongings

1

Report It

Submit a detailed report of what you lost or found — upload a photo, pick your campus, and describe the item so the right person can identify it.

2

Smart Match

Our algorithm matches lost items with found items by category and campus — so you see the most relevant results first, automatically.

3

Claim & Retrieve

Submit a claim, chat directly with the finder through our secure messaging system, and arrange a handoff — completely within the platform.
